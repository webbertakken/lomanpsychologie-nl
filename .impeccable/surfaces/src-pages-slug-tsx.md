# Site surface brief

Scope: every page rendered by `src/pages/[[...slug]].tsx` (home, content pages, treatment pages) plus 404. Mode: Persuade on home (a referred visitor decides to register), Read on every other page.

Audience and job: an adult referred by their huisarts, on a phone at home, often in the evening, not feeling well; they need to know whether they can register now, how, and what it costs. Huisartsen check wait time and contracts.

Proof on hand: real portrait, the practice's own words, tariff tables, insurer lists, folder and aanmeldformulier PDFs, LVVP/NIP memberships. No testimonials.

Constraints: logo unchanged; all copy from Contentful; WCAG 2.2 AA; zero CLS; light and dark.

Memorable moment: the appointment card in the first viewport, a filled-in practice card with tappable phone, e-mail, the registration form and the route to the current waiting time.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the practice's own paperwork made kind: folder, appointment card and form, filled in clearly. It refuses the therapist template of a split hero over a row of service cards and the moody photo hero.

OWN-WORLD: White card stock ruled in cyan dropout ink (the logo's cyan), ballpoint blue-black ink for text, rust from the logo's subline as the only stamp colour for status. Literata sets headings like a typed fill-in; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next sets labels and prose. Flat: nothing overlaps, never shadows. Dark scheme is the carbon copy: blue-black sheet, pale cyan ruling.

STORY: Visitors meet Anke in her words, see at once how to register and whether there is a wait, then read each topic as a labelled field on one form, and leave with phone, mail and crisis numbers in hand.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ruled sheet. Left columns: name and role as the h1, her quote, then the page index. Right 5 columns: a square portrait with the appointment card (aanmeldstop or guidance, Wachttijd, Mailen, Bellen) directly under it, same width, never overlapping (the practice owner's choice). The header carries the phone number and Contact in the first viewport. Mobile: name with a small portrait beside it, quote, card, index.

FORM: fused grounded candidate 4 (Dutch healthcare paperwork: folder, afsprakenkaart, aanmeldformulier). seed:2859733e1420c5bc
Raises: emission-line rail, state by line form not hue (today in office hours is a doubled rule); cutting bench, one ruling governs all spacing; timetable rack, tables set as timetables with tabular figures; panels share edges, nothing overlaps.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
